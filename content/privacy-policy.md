At **JivikaHunt** (accessible from https://jivikahunt.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and how your data is protected.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

- JivikaHunt does not offer public account registration. Job seekers do not need to create an account, upload a resume, or supply sensitive financial credentials to browse jobs.
- When applying for a job, you are directed directly to the employer's official website. We do not store or process your job applications.

---

## 2. Information We May Collect

We may collect minimal non-personally identifiable information to keep the site fast, reliable, and secure:

1. **Log Files & Network Data**: Standard server logs containing Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, referring pages, timestamps, and page request counts. IP addresses are hashed using a cryptographic salt before rate limiting or audit recording.
2. **Local Storage**: We may use browser `localStorage` locally on your device to save bookmarked job listings without requiring a server login. This information stays entirely within your browser.
3. **Contact Inquiries**: If you email us at `contact@jivikahunt.com`, we receive your email address and message solely to respond to your inquiry.

---

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

JivikaHunt uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, remember basic preferences, and serve advertisements in accordance with data protection regulations.

### Types of Cookies We Use

- **Strictly Necessary Cookies**: Required for administrative authentication (`jh_session` httpOnly cookie for authorized contributors and administrators).
- **Preference Cookies**: To store your consent preferences regarding optional third-party integrations.
- **Advertising Cookies**: Used by advertising partners such as Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements based on visits to this and other websites.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain site functionalities.

---

## 4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

JivikaHunt displays advertisements provided by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to support the free operation of this platform.

- **Google as a Third-Party Vendor**: Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to our users based on their visits to JivikaHunt and other sites on the Internet.
- **Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads**: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or through [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- **Cookie Consent**: In compliance with applicable regulations, advertising and analytics scripts are initialized only following consent where legally required.

## 5. External Career Portal Links

Our website contains outbound links to external company career pages and applicant tracking systems. JivikaHunt has no control over and assumes no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites or services.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

JivikaHunt does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records.
