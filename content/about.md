# About JivikaHunt

Welcome to **JivikaHunt**, a dedicated career discovery portal specifically designed for college freshers, graduating students, and early-career job seekers across India.

---

## Our Mission

Finding entry-level jobs and internships in India is often overwhelmed by noisy aggregators, expired links, and third-party recruitment agencies charging fees.

**JivikaHunt was built with a single goal:**
To provide a fast, clean, and 100% verified job discovery platform where freshers and interns can browse genuine opportunities and apply directly on the official career portals of hiring companies.

---

## What Sets Us Apart

- **Direct Careers Portal Links**: We never act as a middleman. Every listing links directly to the hiring company's official job post (`rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer"`).
- **Zero Spam & No Application Fees**: We do not charge candidates, nor do we promote opportunities that require payment or security deposits.
- **Accurate Expiry Tracking**: Deadlines are rigorously maintained according to Indian Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata). Expired postings are promptly retired to respect your time.
- **Distraction-Free Speed**: Fast, lightweight browsing without heavy tracking scripts, intrusive overlays, or popups.
