# 10 — Contributor Guide

## 1. Welcome to the JivikaHunt Team

As a contributor, you play a vital role in curating genuine, high-quality job opportunities for fresh graduates and college students across India.

## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Post a Job

1. **Log In**: Navigate to `/admin/login` and log in with your email and password.
2. **Open Job Editor**: Click "+ New Job" in the dashboard.
3. **Use the ChatGPT Helper (Optional)**:
   - On the right side of the editor, click "Copy ChatGPT Prompt".
   - Paste the raw job posting text from LinkedIn or company careers portals into ChatGPT.
   - Paste ChatGPT's structured output into the form fields.
4. **Fill Mandatory Fields**:
   - **Job Title**: Clear and descriptive (e.g. "Software Engineer Intern").
   - **Company Name**: Official legal company brand.
   - **Type**: Select Fresher, Intern, or Experienced.
   - **Work Mode**: Select Onsite, Hybrid, or Remote.
   - **Locations**: Add at least one city.
   - **Summary**: Concise overview (minimum 40 characters).
   - **Apply Link**: Direct, unshortened HTTPS URL to the company's career portal or ATS form.
5. **Publish**: Click "Publish" to make the listing live instantly, or "Save Draft" to complete later.

## 3. Important Posting Guidelines

- **No URL Shorteners**: Never use `bit.ly`, `tinyurl.com`, or link redirection services. Always use the destination career portal URL.
- **Daily Limit**: Each contributor can publish up to 30 new jobs per 24-hour day.
- **Respect Deadlines**: If the job posting states a last date to apply, enter it in the "Last Date" field so the system automatically handles expiry.
- **WhatsApp Broadcast**: After publishing, click the "Copy post text" button on your job card to copy a pre-formatted broadcast message for WhatsApp channels.
