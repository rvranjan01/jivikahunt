# 10 — Contributor Guide

## 1. Welcome to the JivikaHunt Team

As a contributor, you curate genuine, high-quality job opportunities for fresh graduates and college students across India. You publish instantly with no approval delays.

## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Post a Job

1. **Log In**: Navigate to `/admin/login` and log in with your email and password.
2. **Open Job Editor**: Click **+ New Job** in the top navigation.
3. **Use the ChatGPT AI Helper (Recommended)**:
   - In the right-hand panel, click **Copy ChatGPT Prompt**.
   - Open ChatGPT, paste the prompt, and paste the raw job description from LinkedIn or a company careers page.
   - ChatGPT will extract the structured fields (title, company, type, mode, batches, summary, apply link).
   - Copy-paste the structured fields into the form.
4. **Fill Mandatory Fields**:
   - **Job Title**: Clear and descriptive (e.g. "Graduate Engineer Trainee").
   - **Company Name**: Official legal company brand.
   - **Type**: Select Fresher (0-1 yrs), Intern, or Experienced.
   - **Work Mode**: Select Onsite, Hybrid, or Remote.
   - **Locations**: Add at least one city (comma-separated if multiple, e.g. "Bengaluru, Pune").
   - **Summary**: Concise overview (minimum 40 characters).
   - **Official Apply Link**: Direct, unshortened HTTPS URL to the company's career portal or ATS form.
5. **Publish**: Click **Publish Immediately** to make the listing live instantly, or **Save as Draft** to finish later.

## 3. Important Posting Guidelines

- **Strictly No URL Shorteners**: Never use `bit.ly`, `tinyurl.com`, `t.co`, or redirect services. Always link directly to the official company careers page. Shorteners are automatically blocked by the system.
- **Daily Quota**: Each contributor can publish up to 30 new jobs per 24-hour day.
- **Respect Deadlines**: If the job posting states a last date to apply, enter it in the "Last Date" field so the system automatically computes expiry at 23:59:59 IST on that day.
- **WhatsApp Broadcast Helper**: In `/admin/jobs`, click the **WhatsApp** button on any job card to instantly copy pre-formatted, bolded text with emojis ready to paste into WhatsApp channels and student communities.
- **Duplicate Warnings**: If you attempt to publish a job with the same company, title, and apply URL as an existing listing, the system will display a warning. You can override it by clicking "Publish Anyway" if it is a re-hiring drive.

### Guide Outline 1: "How to Build an ATS-Friendly Resume for College Freshers"

- **Target Audience**: College final-years and recent graduates with 0-1 years of experience.
- **Section 1: Why ATS Rejects 70% of Freshers' Resumes**: Explaining keyword matching, multi-column layout pitfalls, and parsing errors.
- **Section 2: Ideal One-Page Resume Structure**:
  - Header: Name, Location, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub (no physical address, photo, or marital status).
  - Education: Degree, College Name, CGPA/Percentage, Graduation Year.
  - Technical Skills: Languages, Frameworks, Developer Tools, Core CS Subjects.
  - Projects: 2-3 highlighted projects using the XYZ Formula (_Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]_).
  - Internships / Training: Action verbs, technologies used, tangible outcomes.
  - Certifications & Achievements: Hackathons, coding platform ratings (LeetCode/CodeChef).
- **Section 3: Formatting Rules**: Standard fonts, margins, bullet counts, single-page limit, and saving as PDF.

### Guide Outline 5: "7 Red Flags to Spot in Fake Job Offers & Recruitment Scams"

- **Target Audience**: Freshers vulnerable to recruitment fraud.
- **Section 1: The Golden Rule**: Legitimate companies NEVER charge application fees, training fees, or security deposits.
- **Section 2: Top 7 Warning Signs**:
  1. Requests for money (laptop deposit, training fees, background check fees).
  2. Offers issued via WhatsApp or Telegram without any interview rounds.
  3. Free webmail sender addresses (`@gmail.com` claiming to be Microsoft or TCS).
  4. Vague job descriptions with unrealistic salaries (e.g. ₹80,000/month for data entry).
  5. Urgent pressure to transfer funds via UPI.
  6. Poorly formatted offer letters with grammatical errors and blurry logos.
  7. Unverified third-party consultancy middlemen.
- **Section 3: How to Verify & Report**: Checking official career portals and reporting suspicious postings on JivikaHunt.
